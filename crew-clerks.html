CrewClerks · Voice-powered back office for service businesses

CrewClerks is a voice-first workflow platform designed for service businesses. It turns natural spoken or typed job updates into organized business actions such as customer and job records, scheduling, labor, materials, reminders, billing records, proposals, paperwork, and translated communication.

THE PROBLEM

Real work does not arrive neatly formatted.

Service professionals work while moving between customers, job sites, vehicles, phone calls, materials, schedules, and paperwork. Details end up in memory, text messages, photos, handwritten notes, and disconnected systems. Measurements can be forgotten, materials underbilled, callbacks missed, and estimates or invoices delayed.

THE SOLUTION

Capture once. Structure the rest.

CrewClerks captures a spoken or typed update once, identifies the customer and exact job, separates the update into structured work items, asks only for essential missing information, and presents one organized review before anything consequential is finalized.
